# 🌿 NurtureLoop

## Care should continue.

**NurtureLoop** is a patient-focused maternal and child health continuity companion designed to keep care connected across instructions, referrals, caregivers, language, connectivity, and follow-ups.

> **Understand · Connect · Continue**

---

## 💡 The Problem

Maternal and child healthcare can become fragmented after a patient leaves a clinic or hospital.

Care instructions, referrals, appointments, follow-ups, caregiver support, and communication can exist as separate pieces of information. Language barriers and unreliable connectivity can make continuity even harder.

The result is a simple but important gap:

> **Patients may know what happened today, but may not clearly know what happens next.**

NurtureLoop is designed to bridge that continuity gap through one patient-controlled care journey.

---

# 🚀 Our Solution

NurtureLoop brings multiple parts of the care experience into one connected interface.

Instead of treating healthcare as isolated appointments, NurtureLoop represents care as a continuous journey:

## Care should speak your language.

SakhiVoice provides a voice-based interaction layer for approved care information.

* 🇬🇧 English
* 🇮🇳 தமிழ்

# 🔐 Safety by Design

## Assistive, Not Diagnostic

NurtureLoop is designed as a patient-focused assistive platform.

The prototype does **not** aim to provide:

* ❌ Diagnosis
* ❌ Treatment recommendations
* ❌ Clinical decision support
* ❌ Clinical risk scoring
* ❌ Interpretation of medical data
* ❌ Autonomous clinical advice

Instead, the platform focuses on:

* Understanding approved information
* Organizing the care journey
* Connecting referrals
* Coordinating trusted caregivers
* Supporting communication
* Improving continuity
* Keeping patients in control

> **Technology supports the journey. Healthcare professionals remain responsible for clinical decisions.**
